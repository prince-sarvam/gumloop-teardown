# Gumloop Founder Teardown

Five days inside Gumloop's agent app with a real free-tier account: what works, what bills, and what hides. Every claim links to its evidence.

**Open the site:** https://prince-sarvam.github.io/gumloop-teardown/

- 12 findings, a final falsifier scoreboard, the raw founder journal (5 sessions), an evidence browser over 162 captures, and the object and cost ledgers.
- 150 of 162 captures are published; 12 are withheld (the account owner's personal data); the account email and bank-message content are redacted.
- A static single-page app: one `index.html`, no external assets, no tracking. Built from a private research workspace.

## Run locally

    python3 -m http.server 8000

Then open http://localhost:8000/
